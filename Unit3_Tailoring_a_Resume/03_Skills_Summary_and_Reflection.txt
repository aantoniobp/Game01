------------------------------------

   that document client activities, progress, and behaviors. I also review notes for
   accuracy before submission. On an ATS resume, keywords such as documentation,
   recordkeeping, proofreading, case files, and accuracy help the system recognize
   this match.

2) Confidentiality and professional ethics
   Job posting needs: handle confidential/sensitive information with discretion.
   My experience: I maintain confidential client records and follow service plans and
   agency procedures. On an ATS resume, phrases like confidentiality, professional
   ethics, sensitive information, and following procedures signal readiness for legal
   settings where privacy is required.

3) Written and verbal client communication
   Job posting needs: communicate with clients, courts, attorneys, and outside
   organizations; gather information professionally.
   My experience: I communicate with families and support professionals to clarify
   needs and coordinate updates. On an ATS resume, keywords such as client
   communication, written communication, verbal communication, and information
   gathering strengthen the match to legal assistant duties.


ATS REFLECTION PARAGRAPH (for submission)
-----------------------------------------
The three skills I matched from the Entry-Level Paralegal / Legal Assistant posting—
accurate documentation and recordkeeping, confidentiality and professional ethics,
and written and verbal client communication—directly strengthen my ATS resume for
this role. In my CLS / Respite Provider position, I already prepare timely service
documentation, protect confidential client information, and communicate clearly with
families and support professionals. By placing those exact keywords in my Professional
Summary, Skills section, and present-tense experience bullets, an Applicant Tracking
System is more likely to score my resume as a strong match. These skills also connect
to paralegal work because legal teams rely on accurate records, strict confidentiality,
and professional client communication. Tailoring my wording to the posting—rather than
using a generic resume—shows intentional alignment and improves both ATS keyword
recognition and recruiter readability.
