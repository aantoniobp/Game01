POSITION TITLE
Entry-Level Paralegal / Legal Assistant

EMPLOYER
Metro Detroit Legal Support (sample posting for coursework)
Location: Dearborn / Metro Detroit, MI | Hybrid

ABOUT THE ROLE
We are seeking an entry-level Paralegal / Legal Assistant to support attorneys with
case preparation, document organization, and client communication. This role is ideal
for a Criminal Justice or Legal Studies student who is detail-oriented, professional,
and committed to confidentiality.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
- Prepare, proofread, and organize legal documents, correspondence, and case materials
- Maintain accurate electronic and paper case files; update records in a timely manner
- Assist with preliminary legal research and summarize findings for attorney review
- Track deadlines, calendars, and follow-up tasks for active matters
- Communicate professionally with clients, courts, attorneys, and outside organizations
- Gather client information and supporting documents using established procedures
- Handle confidential and sensitive information with discretion and professional ethics
- Support attorneys with administrative tasks and hearing/trial preparation
- Manage multiple assignments while meeting deadlines and maintaining accuracy

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS / SKILLS
1. Strong written and verbal communication skills
2. Exceptional attention to detail and organizational abilities
3. Ability to prepare accurate documentation and maintain confidential records
4. Ability to follow policies, procedures, and attorney direction
5. Comfort with Microsoft Office, email, and electronic file systems
6. Professional judgment and client service skills
7. Dependability and ability to manage competing priorities

PREFERRED / HELPFUL
- Coursework or degree progress in Criminal Justice, Paralegal Studies, or related field
- Transferable experience in documentation, client/family communication, or regulated
  service settings
- Interest in legal research, case support, and court processes
